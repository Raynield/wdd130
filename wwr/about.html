Rocky Mountain Whitewater Rafting

We provide exciting whitewater rafting adventures with professional guides, high-quality equipment, and a strong commitment to safety for all skill levels.

History

Founded in 2015, Rocky Mountain Whitewater Rafting began with a small group of river enthusiasts. Today we guide hundreds of guests each year through some of the most beautiful and challenging rivers in the region.

Our commitment to preservation and high-end outdoor experiences has earned us top ratings. We continue to expand our routes while ensuring safety remains our number one priority.
