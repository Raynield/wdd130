Beginner River Float

Difficulty: Easy (Class I-II)

Perfect for families, first-timers, and nature lovers. Enjoy gentle splashes, beautiful riverside wildlife, and a relaxed 2-hour journey down calm waters guided by experts.

Mountain Challenge

Difficulty: Moderate (Class III-IV)

Step up your adventure with continuous rolling waves, exciting drops, and technical channels. This 4-hour expedition requires teamwork and guarantees thrills!

Extreme Expedition

Difficulty: Advanced (Class IV-V)

A full-day adrenaline rush designed for experienced rafters. Tackle massive hydraulic drops, roaring chutes, and rugged canyon terrain with our veteran river guides.
